Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md · CONDITIONAL PASS · 2 critical, 7 high, 9 medium, 5 low

The fiction-pure + outside-fiction-settings posture is coherent and worth keeping; delivery fidelity fails — guarantees asserted but not authored, and one spine-level silent exclusion. Block Finalize until ACC-1, ACC-2, H-4, V-4/VE-1, SET-1 land. All have now been resolved into the spines.

critical

ACC-1 · Non-sighted visitors silently excluded both directions

EXPERIENCE · Accessibility / Foundation
Auditory-core room, canvas with no text alternative, no claim and no disclaimer.
Fix applied: honesty bullet + M1 non-sighted spike gate + site-page disclosure + canvas entry label & skip link.
critical

ACC-2 · Occluder law deletes rung 2 exactly where rung 1 works

EXPERIENCE · Accessibility vs Soundscape
